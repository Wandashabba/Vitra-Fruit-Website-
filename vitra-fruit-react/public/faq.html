Orders & Shipping

We aim to dispatch all orders within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Delivery nationwide typically takes 1–5 business days depending on your location. Major metropolitan areas (Cape Town, Johannesburg, Durban, Pretoria) are usually at the faster end. You will receive a tracking number by email once your order has been collected by our courier.

We charge a flat-rate shipping fee of R150 on all orders. Orders totalling R500 or more qualify for free standard delivery anywhere in South Africa. Shipping costs are displayed at checkout before you confirm payment. For more detail, visit our Shipping Costs page.

Not yet — we currently deliver within South Africa only. We are actively exploring international shipping options and hope to expand to neighbouring SADC countries and beyond in the near future. Sign up for our newsletter or follow us on Instagram @Vitrafruits to be the first to know when international shipping becomes available.

Yes, collection can be arranged. Please contact us before placing your order so we can confirm availability and arrange a suitable collection time and location. Note that collection orders are not subject to the shipping fee.
